CIA TRIAD
C is for confidentiality
it refers to making sure the data we have is confidential and accessible only to the authorised end points.

I is for Integrity
it refers to making sure the data is accurate and consistent in such a way that no unauthorised acccess is allowed making sure that the data is not changed or influenced.

A is for availability
here we make sure the data is always available to all the authorised end points.
